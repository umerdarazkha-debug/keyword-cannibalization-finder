Keyword cannibalization candidates

These are candidates, not verdicts. The export shows that two pages got impressions for the same query. It cannot show why.

  • Two of your pages listed on the same results page is a double listing, not a problem.
  • Search intent is not in the data. A guide and a product page can both deserve to rank.
  • Language folders and brand searches produce false hits. Filter them out when you run the script.
